# Evidence Document: FDA_LIPITOR_LABEL_SEC7
Source: US FDA Lipitor (atorvastatin calcium) Prescribing Information Section 7.1 & 12.3

EXCERPT:
"Azithromycin does not inhibit CYP3A4 and does not significantly increase atorvastatin exposure. Co-administration of atorvastatin with azithromycin does not require dose adjustment.
In clinical studies, azithromycin 500 mg daily for 3 days did not alter the plasma concentrations of atorvastatin or its active metabolites.
In contrast, strong CYP3A4 inhibitors (clarithromycin, itraconazole, ketoconazole, nefazodone, HIV protease inhibitors) increase atorvastatin AUC by up to 4-fold and Cmax by up to 2-fold, significantly increasing the risk of myopathy and rhabdomyolysis.
Atorvastatin dose should not exceed 20 mg daily when co-administered with clarithromycin."
