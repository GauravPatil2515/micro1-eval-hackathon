# Evidence Document: FDA_PLAVIX_LABEL_BOXED_WARNING
Source: US FDA Prescribing Information Plavix (clopidogrel bisulfate), NDA 020839
Sections: Boxed Warning (Diminished Antiplatelet Effect in Patients with Impaired CYP2C19 Function), Section 7.1

EXCERPT:
"WARNING: DIMINISHED ANTIPLATELET EFFECT IN PATIENTS WITH IMPAIRED CYP2C19 FUNCTION
The effectiveness of Plavix depends on its activation to an active metabolite by Cytochrome P450 (CYP) system, principally CYP2C19.
Clopidogrel is an inactive prodrug that requires a two-step hepatic bioactivation process. CYP2C19 contributes to both steps.
Avoid concomitant use of Plavix with drugs that inhibit CYP2C19, such as omeprazole or esomeprazole. Co-administration of omeprazole 80 mg once daily with clopidogrel 300 mg loading dose followed by 75 mg once daily reduced exposure to the active metabolite of clopidogrel by 45% and reduced platelet inhibition (platelet aggregation) by roughly 40%.
Pantoprazole does not exhibit substantial CYP2C19 inhibition and produces significantly less reduction in active metabolite AUC."
